## What P0 gave you

You have already deployed contracts, read state, made transfers and approvals, upgraded a proxy, run tests and verified source. For the next phase, aim for **one minimal, observable trading loop**:

1. understand how a DEX / AMM works;
2. prepare test tokens;
3. approve a **limited** amount (never infinite);
4. simulate a swap first;
5. observe inputs, outputs and the change in reserves;
6. **revoke the allowance**;
7. review the fees and slippage.

## Principles to keep

- **Low cost**: testnet only, budget still $0.
- **Verifiable**: number every new experiment and record its goal, steps, transaction hashes, blocks, outputs and risks.
- **Few approvals**: approve limited amounts and `approve(spender, 0)` right after use.
- **Simulate locally first**: a passing simulation is not an on-chain result; write the two up separately.
- Keep phase 1 as the "2026-11-12 historical snapshot" and **never overwrite old evidence with new results**; keep theory, project evidence, assumptions and open questions apart.
- Never present testnet amounts, passing tests or source verification as earnings or an audit guarantee.
