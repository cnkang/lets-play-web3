## What Phase-0 gave you

The records so far show deployments, state reads, transfers and <Term id="approve">approvals</Term>, <Term id="proxy">proxy</Term> upgrades, tests and source checks. You can inspect them even if you have not sent a transaction yourself. The next phase plans **one minimal, observable swap workflow**:

1. understand how a <Term id="dex-amm">DEX / AMM</Term> works;
2. prepare <Term id="test-token">test tokens</Term>;
3. approve a **limited** amount (never infinite);
4. <Term id="simulate">simulate</Term> a swap first;
5. observe inputs, outputs and the change in reserves;
6. **revoke the allowance**;
7. review the fees and slippage.

## Principles to keep

- **Low cost**: <Term id="testnet">testnet</Term> only, budget still $0.
- **Verifiable**: number every new experiment and record its goal, steps, <Term id="tx-hash">transaction hashes</Term>, blocks, outputs and risks.
- **Few approvals**: approve limited amounts and `approve(spender, 0)` right after use.
- **Simulate locally first**: a passing simulation is not an on-chain result; write the two up separately.
- Preserve Phase-0’s "2026-11-12 historical snapshot" and date later evidence separately with its block number. **Never overwrite old evidence**; distinguish principles, on-chain results, local tests and future plans.
- Never present testnet amounts, passing tests or source verification as earnings or an <Term id="audit">audit</Term> guarantee.
