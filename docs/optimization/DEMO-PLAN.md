# Demonstration plan

A demonstration shows the labs and the evidence boundary. It does not send a transaction and does not connect a wallet.

1. Open the English or Chinese homepage. Read the three paths aloud: start from zero, play in the browser, learn with an agent.
2. Open the allowance lab. Approve 400. Point at the unchanged balances. Spend 150. Revoke. Point at the tokens that stayed with the recipient.
3. Open the pool lab. Quote 1,000 and 200,000 against the deep pool. Say that the larger price impact is already inside the quote. Turn on the earlier trade, keep the tight tolerance, and show the refusal.
4. Open the hash lab. Edit block 2. Show the failed check. Recalculate forward. Show that the new head is not the head saved at the start. Say that this is not consensus.
5. Open Chapter 10 or Chapter 16 only to show a recorded transaction that the lab did not create.
6. If there is time, copy one AI prompt and show the safety lines. Do not paste a seed phrase, even a fake one, into a live agent during the demo.

Stop. Do not open a faucet, a mainnet site, or a wallet.
