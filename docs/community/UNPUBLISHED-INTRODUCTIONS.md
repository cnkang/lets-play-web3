## Short English introduction

Let’s Play Web3 is a bilingual book plus three browser labs. You can practice an allowance, a constant-product swap, and a SHA-256 chain without a wallet or real money. The labs are simulations. The chapters keep the recorded evidence. https://letsplayweb3.liukang.com/en/

## Longer English introduction

Let’s Play Web3 started as a bilingual record of Sepolia experiments and later local Anvil forks. It now also has three resettable browser labs. One shows that approve writes an allowance and does not move balances. One shows a constant-product quote, with price impact inside the quote and slippage as a minimum the swap must still meet. One hashes a short chain with SHA-256 and shows that a privately recalculated chain is not consensus. Nothing is signed. Where a chapter cites a transaction, that transaction is the evidence, and the lab is practice. https://letsplayweb3.liukang.com/en/labs/
